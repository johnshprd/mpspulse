Welcome to MPS Pulse
MPS Pulse helps you build more muscle by timing your protein correctly — not just hitting a daily total.
⚡
Set Your Goal
Set your daily protein goal at the top. A good target is 1.6–2.2g per kg of bodyweight. Your 4 pulse targets will be distributed automatically.
🕐
Space Your Pulses
Each meal triggers a Muscle Protein Synthesis window lasting ~2–3 hours. Space your 4 pulses at least 3 hours apart for best results.
💪
Hit Every Pulse
Aim for 30–50g per pulse. Going over doesn't help — your body can only use so much per meal. Consistency across all 4 pulses is what drives results.
