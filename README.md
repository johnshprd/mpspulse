# MPS Pulse

**Train your protein, not just your muscles.**

MPS Pulse is a subscription web app that tracks protein intake by muscle protein synthesis (MPS) window — not just daily totals. Most athletes eat enough protein. Almost none eat it at the right times.

## The Science

Muscle protein synthesis peaks ~30–90 minutes after a meal and runs for roughly 2–3 hours before returning to baseline — regardless of how much protein you eat beyond ~40g. Spacing 3–4 protein "pulses" of 30–50g evenly across the day maximises total synthesis time and ensures every gram works harder.

## Features

- 4 timed protein pulse slots (Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner / Evening)
- Live MPS window countdown per slot
- Distribution score — see how evenly your protein is spread
- MPS ceiling alerts when a single meal exceeds ~55g
- Full food library — pulses, meat, and dairy
- Smart serving defaults that learn your habits
- 7-day history
