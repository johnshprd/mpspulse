Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your Pulse account and all associated data at any time.

What gets deleted:

  1. Your account and login credentials
  2. All nutrition and protein tracking data
  3. Custom foods and serving history
  4. Your subscription record (any active subscription will be cancelled)

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. We complete deletion requests within 30 days.

To request deletion, email us from the email address linked to your account:

EMAIL DELETION REQUEST

Or email johnshpd@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and the email address of your account.
